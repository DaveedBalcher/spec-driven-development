Reference answers

The two deliberate ambiguities, and which track surfaced them on the build run

First: whether a transaction belongs to a period by its authorized date or its posted date. The brief says the two are often different days and never says which one counts. Fixture row LK-015, authorized 2026-03-04 and posted 2026-03-05, lands in different periods under the standard cycle depending on the answer. testAuthorizedVsPostedMembership holds one reading.

Second: what a cycle that starts on day 31 does in February. The brief allows any start day from the 1st to the 31st, and the cycle table your setup copied in carries a month-end configuration on the 31st. testFebruaryCycleStart holds one reading.

On the build machine on 2026-09-22, one run per track: OpenSpec found both unprompted, recorded them as blocking open questions without recommending an answer, named LK-015 and the month-end configuration as its evidence, and refused to implement. BMAD found both among five clarifying questions, each with options and a recommendation. LID was handed both answers in its prompt because that run could not stop for a person, so it turned them into requirements rather than questions, and its finding of them is the least independently shown of the three. None of the three chose silently. That is one observation on one machine on one date, not a property of the frameworks: your run is the evidence for your card in Exercise 3.
