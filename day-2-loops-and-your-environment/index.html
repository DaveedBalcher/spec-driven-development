Day 2: Loops and your environment

3 Days of Spec-Driven Development · Day 2 of 3 · four lessons, four exercises, a wrap-up · about three hours: 159 minutes including the opener

Trust, but verify.

a Russian proverb, and the line Ronald Reagan made famous

Yesterday you drove every step: you wrote a spec, read a plan, approved it, and checked the diff yourself. Today you let go of the steering wheel on purpose, which only works if you know exactly what will make the run stop and what you will look at when it does. You will build a runner with three exits and a gate you answer from evidence, put a fresh-context critic on its output and route what it finds without growing your instructions, then write down the environment you actually work in and encode it as permission rules and one narrow write path. Everything today is framework-agnostic; tomorrow you pick a framework.

Starter files
