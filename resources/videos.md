# Recommended videos

One opener per day and one video per lesson, embedded on the lesson pages. None is required to complete an exercise.

**How this list was made.** Candidates came from research notes and YouTube search results; each pick was chosen by recency first and reputation second, from original or conference channels only. Every link was verified mechanically (the video exists, is embeddable, is not a live stream, has an upload date) and its chapters were confirmed against the description on 2026-09-22. `build/checks/check-videos.sh --manifest build/state/videos.json` re-checks them.
